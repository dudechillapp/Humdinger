## Full description (max 4,000 characters, this is 1394)

Got a song stuck in your head but can't name it? Hum it, sing it, beatbox it or mime along to the words you think it has. The Humdinger crowd will name that tune.

HOW IT WORKS
🎤 Record up to 30 seconds: hum, sing, whistle, or film yourself miming along.
🎯 Other people post their guess with a link to the song on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer, SoundCloud, TIDAL or Shazam.
👑 Found it? Crown the right answer. Whoever named it gets 20 points, and everyone else who had a go gets 5.

HAVE A LAUGH TOGETHER
😂 React to clips: Crying, Banger, Brave, Oof or Nailed it.
🎁 Say thanks to whoever named your song, with a message or a recorded thank-you, then keep chatting.
🏆 Climb the leaderboard and level up your character with cooler and cooler kit, from headphones all the way to a golden crown.

REAL PEOPLE, NO AI
No song-recognition robots here. Every answer comes from a real person who knows their music.

NO SIGN-UP NEEDED
Jump straight in with a random fun name like Wobbly Teapot or Lazy Frog. Change it whenever you like, and save your progress with your Google account if you want to keep it.

YOUR CLIPS, YOUR RULES
🗑️ Your recordings delete themselves after the time you choose, from 1 day to 3 months.
🛡️ Report anything that breaks the house rules, block anyone, and delete your account whenever you want.
🙅 No adverts.

Humdinger is for people aged 16 and over.
