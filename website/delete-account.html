Delete your Humdinger account

In the app (quickest)

1. Open Humdinger and go to the Me tab.
2. Scroll down to 🛡️ Safety and privacy.
3. Tap 🗑️ Delete my account, then Delete for good.

Everything is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't use the app any more (for example, you uninstalled it), email humdinger.application@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Humdinger account". Tell us your Humdinger name. If you saved your account with Google, send the email from that Google address so we can check it's you. We'll delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: your name, points, tier, settings and your list of songs you've named.
  • Your requests and recordings, along with every guess, reaction and report on them.
  • Your guesses and comments on other people's requests that haven't been solved yet, and all your replies under guesses and comments on hums.
  • Your friends list (you're also taken off your friends' lists) and any friend requests to or from you.
  • Your thank-yous and chats, including chats with friends (for both people in the conversation), and recordings.
  • Your reactions, your blocked list and any notices for you.
  • Your sign-in account, including the link to your Google account if you saved one.

What's kept

  • If you named or guessed a song that was then solved, your guess stays on that request as "Deleted user", without your comment, so the other person's solved song still makes sense. It's deleted when that request is deleted (at most 3 months after it was posted).
  • Points you helped other people earn stay with them.
  • Records of reports and removals, which don't include your name, are kept for up to 12 months to keep the community safe.
