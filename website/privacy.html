Humdinger

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Humdinger is an app where people hum, sing or mime a song they can't name, and other people name it. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. We've kept it as plain as we can.

Who we are

Humdinger is run by Christopher Kowalski, who is responsible for your data (the "controller" under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation, or GDPR). Contact: humdinger.application@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't have to sign up or give us your email. You start as a guest with a random fun name.
  • Your clips are deleted automatically after the time you choose, at most 3 months.
  • No adverts, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, right in the app.

Microphone, camera and notifications

The app asks to use your microphone and camera only so you can record a clip or a thank-you. It only records while you're recording, and nothing leaves your phone until you tap to post it. Notifications (for example new guesses, comments, replies, your guess being crowned, thank-yous, messages, friend requests, friends' new hums and removed clips) are only sent if you allow them. You can choose exactly which ones you get in the app (Me → Settings), or turn them all off in your phone's settings.

Who can see what

Everyone using Humdinger can see your name, tier, points, and the clips, clues, guesses, comments, replies and reactions you post. Your thank-yous and chats can only be seen by the two people in the conversation. Your friends list, blocked list, reports and notification settings are private. We don't look at conversations unless one is reported to us, and then only to deal with the report.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To run the app for you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), performing our agreement with you): your account, name, points, clips, guesses, messages and settings.
  • To keep Humdinger safe (GDPR Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests): reports, automatic removal of reported clips, blocking, moderation records and security.
  • With your permission (GDPR Article 6(1)(a), consent): using the microphone and camera, and sending notifications. You can withdraw permission in your phone's settings at any time.

Who helps us

Humdinger runs on Google Firebase, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. Google stores and processes the data for us, under Google's data processing terms, on servers in the European Union and the United States (recordings are stored in the United States). Transfers to the United States are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which Google LLC is certified under, and by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Links you add open in the music service you choose (for example YouTube or Spotify), whose own privacy policy then applies. We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable format. You can change your name and your recording settings, and delete your account, right in the app (Me tab). For anything else, email humdinger.application@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live, or ours: the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Germany.

Deleting your account

In the app: Me tab → Safety and privacy → Delete my account. It deletes your profile, clips, guesses, messages and everything else listed on our account deletion page, which also explains how to ask for deletion if you can't use the app. If you're a guest and lose your phone or uninstall the app, you lose access to your account. Email us with your Humdinger name and we'll delete it.

Age

Humdinger is for people aged 16 and over. If you think someone younger is using it, please tell us and we'll delete their account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app.
