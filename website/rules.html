House rules

Humdinger is all about having a laugh together. Everyone agrees to these rules before they post, guess or chat.

😂

Laugh with people, not at them

Everyone's brave for humming in public. Cheer them on. No bullying, mocking people's looks or voices, or harassment.

🚫

No hate

Nothing that attacks people for who they are: race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, where they're from, or anything else.

🧼

Keep it clean

No nudity, sexual content, violence, drugs or anything illegal, in clips, guesses, comments, replies or messages. Comments are for cheering people on or a bit of fun, never for putting someone down.

🙋

Only film yourself

Don't record other people without their permission, and never share anyone's personal details, like phone numbers or addresses. That includes your own.

🎯

Real guesses only

Guesses are honest attempts with a link to the song. No spam, ads, self-promotion or links to anything else.

🎭

Be yourself

Don't pretend to be someone else, including the Humdinger team.

🎂

16 and over

You need to be at least 16 to use Humdinger.

If someone breaks the rules

Every clip, guess, reply and conversation can be reported from its ⋮ menu, and you can block anyone. Blocked people disappear from your feed, can't guess on your requests and can't message you. They aren't told.

A clip reported by three different people (from accounts at least a day old) is removed automatically, and its owner is told why. We also review reports ourselves, and we can remove anything, or any account, that breaks these rules.

To report something urgent, or anything we should know about, email humdinger.application@gmail.com.

Copyright

Humming or singing a few seconds of a song to find out what it's called is what Humdinger is for. If you own the rights to something posted on Humdinger and want it taken down, report it in the app with "Copyright problem", or email humdinger.application@gmail.com with what it is, where it appears in the app (the name of the person who posted it and roughly when), and how we can reach you. We'll remove content that infringes your rights.
